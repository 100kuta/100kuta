### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Geoinformatics and Surveying professional** working at the intersection of **spatial data science, satellite remote sensing, and applied artificial intelligence**. My background bridges the physical and analytical worlds: moving from ground data collection, cadastral surveying, and municipal mapping to spatial modeling, offline language models, and interactive decision-support applications.

- 🌍 **Geospatial & Remote Sensing:** Satellite Earth Observation analysis (Sentinel-5P, MODIS), Google Earth Engine (GEE), QGIS, and spatial exposure modeling.
- 🤖 **Applied AI & Local LLMs:** Air-gapped RAG pipelines, quantized local inference (`llama.cpp`, GGUF), dense/sparse hybrid search (ChromaDB, FAISS, BM25), and prompt engineering.
- 🗺️ **Web & Mobile GIS:** Custom Leaflet mapping platforms, routing machines with dynamic tariff calculation, and enterprise mobile GIS data collection (ODK Collect).
- 🔬 **Computer Vision:** Image forensics and copy-move forgery detection using frequency-domain feature analysis (DCT + PCA).
